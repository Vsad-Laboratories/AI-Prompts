# Analysis Prompt: Competitive Intelligence Synthesis

## Purpose
Synthesize messy, unstructured competitive information, product releases, and market news to reveal competitor strategies, strengths, weaknesses, and potential market gaps.

## Inputs
- `COMPETITOR_DATA`: Articles, pricing pages, social media mentions, feature lists, or press releases.
- `OUR_PRODUCT`: A description of our own offering and strategic goals.

## Instructions
1. Extract key facts, features, and claims from the `COMPETITOR_DATA`.
2. Map the competitor's value proposition against `OUR_PRODUCT`.
3. Construct a **SWOT Analysis** of the competitor based on the extracted information.
4. Estimate the competitor's target segment, monetization model, and likely future product roadmap.
5. Identify **Strategic Opportunities**: Where is the competitor weakest or overlooking customer needs? How can we capitalize on this?

## Constraints
- Base the synthesis purely on objective data. Avoid wishful thinking or underestimating the competitor.
- Clearly separate verified facts from educated guesses or extrapolations.

## Expected output
- **Competitor Profile**: Value proposition, target audience, and pricing/monetization strategy.
- **SWOT Matrix**: In-depth strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
- **Competitive Comparison Matrix**: Feature-by-feature mapping against our own product.
- **Actionable Strategic Gaps**: Specific product/marketing initiatives we should pursue.
