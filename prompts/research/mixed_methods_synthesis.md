# Research Prompt: Mixed-Methods Research Synthesis Protocol

## Purpose
Synthesize qualitative user research (interviews, observational studies) and quantitative telemetry analytics into a unified, triangulated research synthesis.

## Inputs
- `QUALITATIVE_DATA`: User interview transcripts, field observation notes, or survey comments.
- `QUANTITATIVE_DATA`: Usage metrics, conversion funnels, analytics event counts, or error rates.

## Instructions
1. Extract recurring qualitative themes and user behavioral friction points from `QUALITATIVE_DATA`.
2. Extract statistical trends and behavioral drop-offs from `QUANTITATIVE_DATA`.
3. Triangulate qualitative insights against quantitative analytics to identify converging findings, diverging anomalies, and complementary insights.
4. Resolve contradictions where qualitative sentiment conflicts with quantitative user actions.
5. Formulate data-backed product and research recommendations based on the converged findings.

## Constraints
- Avoid over-indexing on vocal minority qualitative feedback if contradicted by large-scale quantitative data.
- Ensure qualitative quotes directly illustrate quantitative data trends.

## Expected output
- **Triangulation Matrix**: Converging, diverging, and complementary research findings table.
- **Synthesized Insights Breakdown**: Combined qualitative-quantitative user behavioral models.
- **Strategic Product Recommendations**: Prioritized action items rooted in triangulated evidence.
