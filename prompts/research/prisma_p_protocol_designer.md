# Research Prompt: Systematic Review Protocol Designer (PRISMA-P Compliant)

## Purpose
Design a rigorous, replicable systematic literature review protocol adhering strictly to the PRISMA-P (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic review and Meta-Analysis Protocols) framework.

## Inputs
- `RESEARCH_TOPIC`: Topic or research question for systematic review.
- `INCLUSION_EXCLUSION_CRITERIA`: Domain boundaries, publication date ranges, language constraints, and study types.

## Instructions
1. Define the primary research question using the **PICO / PICOS** framework (Population, Intervention, Comparator, Outcome, Study design).
2. Construct comprehensive, Boolean search strings for major academic databases (IEEE Xplore, PubMed, ACM Digital Library, Scopus).
3. Establish explicit Eligibility Criteria (Inclusion and Exclusion rules) with rationale.
4. Formulate primary and secondary outcome measures to be extracted from selected studies.
5. Define data extraction schemas and quality assessment tools (e.g., Cochrane Risk of Bias, ROBINS-I).

## Constraints
- Search strings must utilize exact syntax rules (truncation operators, field tags) appropriate for academic search engines.
- Ensure selection criteria prevent publication bias and language bias where possible.

## Expected output
- **PICO Research Framework**: Structured research question components.
- **Database Search Strings**: Validated Boolean search query strings for multiple databases.
- **Inclusion / Exclusion Checklist**: Operational decision criteria for study screening.
- **Data Extraction & Risk of Bias Protocol**: Standardized data collection templates.
