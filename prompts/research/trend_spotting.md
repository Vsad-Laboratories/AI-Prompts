# Research Prompt: Trend Spotting and Foresight Analysis

## Purpose
Examine industry news, patent filings, academic abstracts, or market events to identify emerging macro trends, map their trajectories, and assess their strategic impact.

## Inputs
- `MARKET_SIGNALS`: Bullet points of recent events, scientific breakthroughs, startup fundings, or industry shifts.
- `TARGET_HORIZON`: Timeframe for foresight (e.g., 2 years, 5 years, 10 years).

## Instructions
1. Analyze the `MARKET_SIGNALS` to isolate underlying technical, economic, or cultural drivers.
2. Group related signals into 3 distinct **Trend Vectors** (e.g., decentralized orchestration, automated compliance, resource-efficient compute).
3. For each Trend Vector, analyze its current maturity (Emerging, Accelerating, Mature) and map its potential trajectory over the `TARGET_HORIZON`.
4. Run a **Cross-Impact Analysis**: How will these trends intersect or amplify each other?
5. Formulate 3 strategic recommendations for an organization or product looking to capture these opportunities or hedge against disruption.

## Constraints
- Avoid sci-fi speculation; keep predictions tightly coupled to the evidence present in `MARKET_SIGNALS`.
- Address the counter-trends or friction points (e.g., regulatory hurdles, societal pushback) that could slow down these trends.

## Expected output
- **Trend Taxonomy Board**: Theme, maturity, signals, and primary drivers.
- **Intersection Matrix**: Impact of trend collisions.
- **Foresight Timeline**: Phased roadmap detailing major milestones from year 1 to target horizon.
- **Strategic Hedging Guidelines**: Defensive and offensive actions to take.
