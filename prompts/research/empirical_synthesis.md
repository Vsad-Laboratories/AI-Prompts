# Research Prompt: Empirical Data Synthesis

## Purpose
Synthesize qualitative and quantitative research findings from interviews, user research, or field observations into coherent user insights and archetypes.

## Inputs
- `FIELD_OBSERVATIONS`: Transcripts, notes, quotes, or metric summaries from user studies or field work.
- `RESEARCH_GOAL`: The focus area or product opportunity we are trying to inform.

## Instructions
1. Review the `FIELD_OBSERVATIONS` and tag key recurring concepts, pain points, and behaviors.
2. Construct an **Affinity Diagram / Themes Map**: Group similar findings into 3-5 distinct thematic clusters.
3. Formulate 2-3 detailed user archetypes/personas based purely on the observed data.
4. Extract 3 high-level "Insight Statements" that describe the user's deep underlying needs and why current solutions fail.
5. Provide actionable design or product recommendations mapped directly to each insight statement.

## Constraints
- Do not invent user quotes or exaggerate pains; every user archetype must be tied directly back to evidence in `FIELD_OBSERVATIONS`.
- Clearly separate direct observations from secondary inferences.

## Expected output
- **Thematic Cluster Matrix**: Grouped quotes, behaviors, and observations.
- **Empirical User Personas**: Realistic user profiles with goals, pains, and habits.
- **Insight and Action Roadmap**: Core insights matched with practical product suggestions.
