# Research Prompt: Expert Interview Protocol Designer

## Purpose
Design highly focused, bias-free, and deep expert interview guides to extract technical, structural, or industry insights.

## Inputs
- `RESEARCH_DOMAIN_AND_GOALS`: The scientific topic, product domain, or industrial trend being researched (e.g., challenges in solid-state battery manufacturing).
- `EXPERT_PROFILE_METADATA`: Backstory, technical expertise level, and professional role of the interview targets.

## Instructions
1. Analyze the core research goals in `RESEARCH_DOMAIN_AND_GOALS` to define key learning objectives for the interview session.
2. Formulate open-ended, non-leading questions designed to elicit detailed, experiential responses rather than binary (yes/no) answers.
3. Establish a logical funnel structure: moving from broad industry context, to deep technical challenges, to forward-looking projections.
4. Design targeted follow-up prompts and Socratic probes to investigate expert assertions when they use generic industry terms.
5. Identify potential biases (e.g., confirmation bias, expert overconfidence) and outline question structures to actively mitigate them.

## Constraints
- Ensure the interview protocol is structured to fit standard session limits (e.g., 45 to 60 minutes).
- Do not ask questions that could violate confidentiality or trade secret boundaries.

## Expected output
- **Interview Learning Objectives**: Clarified research targets mapped to the session timeline.
- **Expert Interview Guide (Timeline-Phased)**: The step-by-step interview script with questions and temporal guidelines.
- **Probing & Follow-Up Playbook**: List of technical probes to dive deeper into general statements.
- **Interviewer Bias Mitigation Guide**: Warnings and question adjustments to ensure objective insights.
