# Writing Prompt: Press Release Synthesizer

## Purpose
Synthesize raw product documentation, engineering breakthroughs, or business milestones into professional, media-ready, and SEO-optimized press releases.

## Inputs
- `RAW_LAUNCH_DATA`: Internal wikis, developer notes, strategic pitch decks, or interview transcripts detailing the milestone.
- `TARGET_AUDIENCE_AND_TONE`: The specific publication channel and expected stylistic tone (e.g., TechCrunch-style technical, Wall Street Journal-style financial).

## Instructions
1. Extract the core "Who, What, When, Where, Why" hook from the `RAW_LAUNCH_DATA`.
2. Construct a compelling, high-impact headline and sub-headline that clearly summarize the announcement's value.
3. Organize the press release according to the classic inverted pyramid structure (crucial details first, followed by supporting contexts).
4. Draft professional quotes representing company executives or technical leads, capturing the tone from `TARGET_AUDIENCE_AND_TONE`.
5. Integrate relevant search engine optimization (SEO) terms and keywords naturally into the copy.

## Constraints
- Avoid empty marketing buzzwords or hyperbole; base all claims on raw data.
- Maintain standard AP style formatting throughout the draft.

## Expected output
- **Press Release Headline & Sub-head**: Compelling title options.
- **Inverted Pyramid Lead Paragraph**: The immediate news hook.
- **Full Press Release Body**: Well-structured AP-style news release draft.
- **Media Kit Boilerplate & Quotes**: Standalone company background and representative quotes.
